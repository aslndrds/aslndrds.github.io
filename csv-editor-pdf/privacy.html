CSV Editor & PDF Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

EnglishTürkçe

1. Scope

This policy applies to the CSV Editor & PDF Tools Android application, package com.aslndrds.csveditorpdf, provided by Aydoğdu Studios.

2. Your documents stay local

The app opens CSV, TSV, and PDF documents that you choose through Android's system file picker. Editing, searching, sorting, cleaning, previewing, and PDF, JSON, TSV, or CSV export are performed on your device. We do not operate an account system or upload your document contents to our servers.

Files are shared with another service only when you deliberately use Android's Share or Open with feature and choose that service.

3. Local app data

The app may remember settings, favourites, recent-document references, export preferences, and consent choices on your device. You can remove this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Exported documents must be deleted separately from the location where you saved them.

4. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner and occasional interstitial advertisements. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform may process technical information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Where required, the app displays a Google-certified consent message before requesting ads. If you decline personalised advertising, Google may still serve non-personalised or limited ads where legally permitted. You can revisit your privacy choices from the app's Settings screen.

5. Permissions and access

  • Internet and network state: used for consent management and advertising.
  • Selected files: access is granted through Android's system file picker only for documents you choose.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

6. Children

The app is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

7. Third-party service

For details about Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

8. Contact and updates

We may update this policy when the app, services, or legal requirements change. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to imhotep.mstf@gmail.com.
