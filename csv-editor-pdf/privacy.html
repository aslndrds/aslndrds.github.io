Smart PDF & Sheet Editor Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

EnglishTürkçe

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Smart PDF & Sheet Editor Android application, package com.aslndrds.csveditorpdf, provided by Aydoğdu Studios. The app does not require an account.

2. Local document processing

The app can open, create, edit, scan, search, sign, annotate, redact, compare, convert, preview, and export PDF, spreadsheet, Office, OpenDocument, text, image, and web content selected by you. Document editing, PDF rendering, camera cleanup, OCR, spreadsheet work, conversions, signatures, and recovery data are processed locally on your device unless a feature below explicitly requires a network connection.

We do not operate a document, account, analytics, or cloud-conversion server, and we do not upload your document contents to our servers. Files leave the app only when you deliberately choose Android's Share/Open with feature or enter a web address for web-to-PDF processing.

3. Camera, OCR, and optional downloads

  • Camera: requested only when you open the document scanner, so you can photograph paper documents. Captured pages remain on your device.
  • On-device OCR: ML Kit and the installed Tesseract engine recognize text locally. ML Kit may contact Google for component fixes, model updates, and hardware-accelerator compatibility information; document images and recognized text are not sent to Aydoğdu Studios.
  • OCR language packs: optional packs are downloaded over HTTPS only after you request them. The request identifies the selected language pack, not your document contents, and downloaded files are checksum-verified.
  • Web to PDF: the app loads only the HTTPS address that you explicitly enter. The visited website may process data under its own privacy policy.

4. Local app data

The app may store settings, favourites, recent-document references, export preferences, consent choices, recovery copies, imported fonts, saved visual signatures, certificates you import, and optional OCR packs in app-private storage. Saved signatures are encrypted with Android Keystore-backed encryption. Exported documents are stored in the location you choose.

5. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display one banner on the home screen and limited interstitial advertisements after eligible exports. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform may collect or share IP-derived approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data handled by these services is encrypted in transit.

Where required, the app displays a Google-certified consent message before requesting ads. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily disable all advertising: Google may serve non-personalised or limited ads where legally permitted. You can revisit available privacy choices from the app's Settings screen.

6. Permissions and access

  • Camera: document scanning, only while you use the scanner.
  • Internet and network state: consent management, advertising, requested OCR packs, and user-requested HTTPS web-to-PDF.
  • Selected files: Android's system file picker grants access only to files or locations you choose.

The app does not request contacts, microphone, precise location, account-list, or broad all-files access.

7. Delete your data

No account is created. Aydoğdu Studios does not keep a server-side copy of your documents or local app data.

  1. Delete exported documents from the folder where you saved them.
  2. In the app, delete saved signatures, imported fonts, certificates, recovery items, and downloaded OCR language packs from their relevant management screens when available.
  3. To remove all remaining local app data, open Android Settings → Apps → Smart PDF & Sheet Editor → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall the app.
  4. For a privacy request concerning Aydoğdu Studios, email imhotep.mstf@gmail.com and identify the request as relating to Smart PDF & Sheet Editor. We will respond within 30 days.

Local data is removed immediately when you delete it or clear app storage, subject to Android and storage-provider behavior. We do not retain deleted document data on our servers because it is never uploaded to us. Google may retain advertising or consent data under its own policies and legal obligations; use Google's privacy controls for that data.

8. Security and retention

Cleartext network traffic is disabled. Final document saves use validation and protected replacement where supported. Local data remains until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Files exported outside app-private storage remain until you delete them from that location.

9. Children

The app is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

10. Third-party services

For details about Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

11. Contact and updates

We may update this policy when the app, included services, or legal requirements change. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to imhotep.mstf@gmail.com.

2. Yerel belge işleme

Uygulama; seçtiğiniz PDF, elektronik tablo, Office, OpenDocument, metin, görsel ve web içeriğini açabilir, oluşturabilir, düzenleyebilir, tarayabilir, arayabilir, imzalayabilir, açıklama veya karartma ekleyebilir, karşılaştırabilir, dönüştürebilir ve dışa aktarabilir. Belge düzenleme, PDF görüntüleme, kamera temizleme, OCR, elektronik tablo çalışmaları, dönüşümler, imzalar ve kurtarma verileri; aşağıda açıkça ağ gerektirdiği belirtilen özellikler dışında cihazınızda işlenir.

Belge, hesap, analiz veya bulut dönüşüm sunucusu işletmeyiz ve belge içeriklerinizi kendi sunucularımıza yüklemeyiz. Dosyalar yalnızca Android'in Paylaş/Birlikte aç özelliğini bilerek kullandığınızda veya web'den PDF için bir adres girdiğinizde uygulamanın dışına çıkar.

3. Kamera, OCR ve isteğe bağlı indirmeler

  • Kamera: yalnızca belge tarayıcıyı açtığınızda kâğıt belgeleri fotoğraflamak için istenir. Yakalanan sayfalar cihazınızda kalır.
  • Cihazda OCR: ML Kit ve kurulu Tesseract motoru metni yerel olarak tanır. ML Kit bileşen düzeltmeleri, model güncellemeleri ve donanım hızlandırıcı uyumluluğu için Google ile bağlantı kurabilir; belge görselleri ve tanınan metin Aydoğdu Studios'a gönderilmez.
  • OCR dil paketleri: isteğe bağlı paketler yalnızca siz istediğinizde HTTPS üzerinden indirilir. İstek belge içeriğini değil seçilen dil paketini belirtir; indirilen dosyaların sağlama toplamı doğrulanır.
  • Web'den PDF: yalnızca açıkça girdiğiniz HTTPS adresi yüklenir. Ziyaret edilen site kendi gizlilik politikasına göre veri işleyebilir.

4. Yerel uygulama verileri

Ayarlar, favoriler, son belge bağlantıları, dışa aktarma tercihleri, izin seçimleri, kurtarma kopyaları, eklenen fontlar, kayıtlı görsel imzalar, içe aktardığınız sertifikalar ve isteğe bağlı OCR paketleri uygulamaya özel alanda saklanabilir. Kayıtlı imzalar Android Keystore destekli şifreleme ile korunur. Dışa aktarılan belgeler seçtiğiniz konumda saklanır.

Uygulama ana ekranda bir banner ve uygun dışa aktarma işlemlerinden sonra sınırlı geçiş reklamları göstermek için Google AdMob kullanır. Bu hizmetlerin işlediği veriler aktarım sırasında şifrelenir.

Gerekli bölgelerde uygulama, reklam istemeden önce Google onaylı izin mesajı gösterir.
