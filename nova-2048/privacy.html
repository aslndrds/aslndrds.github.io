Privacy Policy

NOVA 2048 — 2027 is developed by Aydoğdu Studios. This policy explains the information used when you play the Android game.

Information stored on your device

Scores, settings, bonuses, achievements and game progress are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove this local data.

Google account

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose it, the game can access the display name and email address supplied by Google to identify your account. The current release does not sell this information.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob for banner, optional rewarded, limited interstitial and app-open advertising. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions and diagnostics. Where required, the Google User Messaging Platform presents consent and privacy choices. You can reopen advertising privacy choices from the game settings.

Diagnostics and online features

Google services may receive technical diagnostics needed for security, fraud prevention and service delivery. If cloud leaderboards or sync are activated, Firebase may process your account identifier, display name, score and security diagnostics. Online features are optional.

Children

The game does not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children. Age-related advertising treatment and regional requirements will be applied through the store and advertising settings.

Your choices and deletion

You may play without Google sign-in, control advertising consent where available, disable sound and voice, sign out, or remove local data by clearing app storage. To request deletion of online account or leaderboard data, contact us using the support address below.

Third-party services

Service providers operate under their own policies, including Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob policies.
