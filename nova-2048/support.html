PLAYER SUPPORT

How can we help?

Game controls

Tap any tile in a group of two or more matching edge-neighbours. The connected group merges at the selected tile, the result falls and new tiles fill the board. Tap a marked special tile to activate its power. Tap the mode badge at the top to change mode, theme or sound.

Progress and rewards

Progress is saved automatically. Optional rewarded ads are offered when a bonus inventory reaches zero. Daily rewards are available once per day.

Supported languages

NOVA follows the Android system language. English is the fallback. Voice guidance requires the matching Android text-to-speech voice to be installed.

Privacy and data deletion

Advertising privacy choices are available inside the game settings. To request deletion of online account or leaderboard data, email us from the address associated with the request.
